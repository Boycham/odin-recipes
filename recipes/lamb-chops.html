Lamb Chops

Description

These pan-seared garlic and herb lamb rib chops are incredibly tender, deeply savory, and take less than 30 minutes to make.

This recipe features tender, juicy lamb rib chops seared quickly at a high heat to achieve a beautiful, caramelized outer crust while keeping the inside perfectly pink. It is basted generously with a melted butter, garlic, and fresh thyme mixture that infuses the meat with a rich, aromatic flavor, turning a simple cut of meat into an elegant, restaurant-quality dish.

Ingredients

    1 1/2 lbs lamb rib chops (about 6-8 chops, patted dry)
    1 tbsp garlic salt
    1 tbsp coarsely ground black pepper
    1/2 tbsp smoked paprika
    1/2 tbsp brown sugar
    1/4 cup all-purpose flour (for light dredging)
    3 tbsp unsalted butter
    3-4 cloves garlic, smashed
    4-5 sprigs fresh thyme leaves
    2 tbsp olive oil (for frying)

Steps

    Prep the meat: Rinse the lamb chops and pat them completely dry with paper towels. Trim away any excess outer fat if desired.
    Season: In a small bowl, mix the garlic salt, black pepper, smoked paprika, and brown sugar. Rub this spice blend generously onto both sides of the lamb chops.
    Dredge: Lightly press both sides of the seasoned chops into the flour, shaking off any excess.
    Sear: Heat the olive oil in a heavy-bottomed skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Place the chops in the pan without overcrowding them. Cook undisturbed for 3 to 4 minutes until a deep, golden-brown crust forms.
    Flip and baste: Flip the chops over. Immediately add the butter, smashed garlic cloves, and fresh thyme sprigs to the pan.
    Finish: As the butter melts and foams, tilt the pan slightly and use a spoon to continuously pool and ladle the hot herb butter over the top of the lamb chops. Cook for another 3 to 4 minutes until they reach your desired doneness (135°F for medium-rare).
    Rest: Remove the lamb chops from the skillet and place them on a warm plate. Drizzle the remaining pan butter over the top and let the meat rest for 5 minutes before serving.
